Image sources and licensing

## Country flags

Source: https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons

Revision: ac7e0eb3259eb62aa6100c435607e2fe03896a97

Original files: flags/4x3/*.svg and country.json, used to build the name mapping.
The SVG flags are copied unchanged. The generated assets/flags.js adds aliases
for country names used by the TVEvents schedule service, including historic
spellings. The exact upstream MIT copyright and license notice is included in
licenses/flag-icons-MIT.txt. MIT-licensed flags retain their upstream license.

The original asset path for every vendored flag is:
https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons/blob/ac7e0eb3259eb62aa6100c435607e2fe03896a97/flags/4x3/{filename}

## Original plugin icons

assets/images/flags/world.svg, assets/images/ico_tv.svg and
assets/images/ico_prediction.svg are original geometric SVG drawings created
for TVEvents, distributed under the plugin's GPL-2.0-or-later license.
The neutral globe is used for unknown countries, unsupported historical
territories, continents and sports organisations without a matching licensed
flag. It is not a federation or team logo.

All image files are served locally from the installed WordPress plugin.
The legacy PNG flags and icons from unknown sources are not distributed.
